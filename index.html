Project insights — truck & dock simulation

live model

A simplified version of the underlying queueing mechanics: trucks arrive at random (Poisson process), each dock serves one truck at a time with variable service duration, and a truck waits for the next free dock. Adjust the inputs to see how throughput, wait time, and dock utilization respond — this is the same dynamic the full simulation explores at much higher fidelity.
